Privacy Policy

At Vishuddhi Home Garden, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and manage your personal information when you visit our website, create an account, or purchase plants, gardening products, and related items from us.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use our website:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number
  • Billing and delivery address
  • Account login information, where applicable
  • Order history and purchase details
  • Payment transaction information processed through secure payment providers
  • Product reviews, ratings, and feedback you choose to submit
  • Device information, browser type, and IP address
2. How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Process and deliver your plant and gardening product orders.
  • Provide customer support and respond to your enquiries.
  • Send order confirmations, delivery updates, and service notifications.
  • Manage your account, wishlist, cart, and orders.
  • Improve our website, products, and gardening services.
  • Provide relevant plant and gardening product recommendations.
  • Process appointments or enquiries submitted through our website.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
3. Payment Security

Your payment security is important to us. Vishuddhi Home Garden does not intentionally store your complete credit card, debit card, UPI, or banking credentials on our website. Payments are processed through secure payment service providers using appropriate security measures. Payment providers may process transaction information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share limited information when necessary to provide our services, including with:

  • Trusted payment gateway and payment service providers
  • Delivery and logistics partners
  • Technology and website service providers
  • Customer support or service partners where required
  • Government authorities or legal bodies when required by law
5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Vishuddhi Home Garden may use cookies and similar technologies to provide a smooth browsing experience, remember your preferences, maintain shopping cart and account functionality, and understand website usage. You may disable cookies through your browser settings, although certain website features may not function correctly.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to process orders, provide customer support, manage accounts, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information associated with your account.
  • Update or correct inaccurate account information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where legally permitted.
  • Manage your communication and marketing preferences.
  • Contact us regarding questions about the use of your personal information.
8. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, loss, or disclosure. However, no website, online service, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Plant Orders & Customer Information

Information collected when you purchase plants, pots, soil mixes, seeds, fertilizers, gardening accessories, and other home-garden products is used to process your order, arrange delivery, provide customer support, and improve our products and services. Information submitted through product reviews or feedback may be displayed publicly where applicable, without intentionally displaying sensitive personal information.

10. Third-Party Services

Our website may use or provide links to third-party services, including payment gateways, delivery providers, analytics services, social media platforms, or external tracking portals. These third parties may have their own privacy policies and terms. Vishuddhi Home Garden is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites or services that we do not control.

11. Children's Privacy

Vishuddhi Home Garden's website and online purchasing services are intended for individuals who are legally able to enter into purchases and transactions. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for independent purchases. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information to us, they may contact us to request appropriate action.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Vishuddhi Home Garden may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, business practices, or applicable legal requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically for the latest information.

13. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy shall be governed by and interpreted in accordance with the applicable laws of India. Any disputes relating to the use of our website or services shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the competent courts in India.
