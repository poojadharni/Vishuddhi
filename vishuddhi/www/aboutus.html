About Us

A Family-Run Nursery Helping You Bring the Right Plants Home.

Welcome to Vishuddhi Home Garden

Vishuddhi Home Garden is a family-run nursery based in Bengaluru, helping plant lovers find the right plants for their homes, balconies, terraces, and gardens.

We believe choosing a plant should be simple and personal. Instead of simply selling a plant, we help you understand what will work best for your space, sunlight, and lifestyle. From healthy indoor and outdoor plants to pots, soil mixes, fertilisers, and essential gardening tools, we bring everything you need to create a thriving green space at home.

Our approach is built around honest guidance, fresh products, and continued support. Whether you're choosing your first plant or adding to an existing garden, we're here to help you make the right choice.

Our Vision

To help more people create healthy, beautiful green spaces by making plant selection, gardening, and plant care simple, practical, and accessible.

Our Mission

To provide healthy plants, fresh soil mixes, suitable pots, and essential gardening products while giving every customer honest guidance and ongoing support to help their plants thrive.
